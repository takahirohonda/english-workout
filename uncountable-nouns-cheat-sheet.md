# 🧠 Uncountable Nouns Cheat Sheet

These nouns **don't take "a/an"** and usually use **singular verbs**.

## 🔹 Common Categories and Examples

| **Category**         | **Uncountable Nouns**                                  | **Use Instead**                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Information**      | advice, news, information, knowledge, data             | a piece of advice, a bit of news, a fact            |
| **Weather**          | rain, snow, sunshine, thunder, weather                 | a spell of rain, a drop of snow, a storm            |
| **Food/Drink**       | bread, rice, water, cheese, coffee, meat, fruit, sugar | a slice of bread, a cup of coffee, a piece of fruit |
| **Abstract Ideas**   | love, happiness, anger, fear, fun, beauty, freedom     | a feeling of love, a moment of fun                  |
| **Subjects/Fields**  | math, physics, economics, politics, history            | _(usually no need to count these)_                  |
| **Materials**        | wood, glass, paper, metal, plastic, gold               | a sheet of paper, a piece of wood                   |
| **Work/Tasks**       | work, homework, research, progress, housework          | a task, a project, a piece of homework              |
| **Money/Time**       | money, time, currency, change                          | a dollar, a minute, a coin                          |
| **Travel/Transport** | traffic, luggage, baggage, travel, transport           | a bag, a trip, a suitcase                           |

---

## 🔹 Common Mistakes and Fixes

| ❌ **Incorrect**  | ✅ **Correct**                         |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| a good advice     | some good advice / a piece of advice   |
| many informations | much information / some pieces of info |
| a furniture       | a piece of furniture                   |
| the news are bad  | the news is bad                        |

---

## 🔹 Grammar Tips

- ✅ Use **“some”** if you don’t need to be specific:

  - “I got some great advice.”
  - “She gave me some feedback.”

- ✅ Use **“a piece of…”** or other counters for specific amounts:

  - “A piece of luggage”
  - “A slice of cake”
  - “A bit of luck”

- ✅ Use **singular verbs** with uncountable nouns:
  - “The information **is** useful.”
  - “The furniture **was** expensive.”

---

## 🔹 How to Practice

Try replacing incorrect usage in sentences like these:

> ❌ I got a good news yesterday.  
> ✅ I got some good news yesterday.

> ❌ He gave me many advices.  
> ✅ He gave me some advice. / He gave me a piece of advice.

> ❌ The furniture are modern.  
> ✅ The furniture is modern.

---

## ✅ Bonus: Quick Reference

- “Much” not “many” → **much information**, **much advice**
- “Some” is your friend → **some water**, **some progress**, **some bread**
- Count with phrases → **a piece of**, **a bit of**, **a cup of**, **a slice of**
