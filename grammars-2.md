## Which one is grammatically correct?

<details>
<summary>
1. plastics

(A) Burning plastics releases toxic fumes.
(B) Burning the plastics releases toxic fumes.

</summary>

**Answer** <br/>

both (A) and (B) are grammatically correct, but they are used in slightly different contexts:

✅ (A) "Burning plastics releases toxic fumes."
This is a general statement about plastics—true in general, no specific plastics are being referred to.

✅ (B) "Burning the plastics releases toxic fumes."
This is also correct, but it refers to specific plastics that have already been mentioned or are known from context.
Example: "We found some old bottles in the shed. Burning the plastics releases toxic fumes."

</details>
